[b]1.2[/b]
[list]
[*]An auto-swap no longer forces the pawn back onto the target it had before the swap. Pawns retarget normally instead of charging the enemy they were shooting at, or emptying a magazine into a downed one. An attack you ordered by hand is still resumed.
[*]Weapons another mod stows in a pawn's inventory, such as Grab Your Tool! swapping to a tool for a job, are now carried as sidearms, so the swap gizmo no longer disappears once the job is done.
[/list]

[b]1.1[/b]
[list]
[*]Sidearms are no longer hauled off when a pawn's inventory is unloaded, so a caravan coming home keeps its sidearms.
[*]Manage Sidearm Policies support: weapons a policy puts in a colonist's inventory count as sidearms here, and sidearms you pick up by hand fulfill the policy's slots instead of it fetching a duplicate.
[/list]

[b]1.0[/b]
[list]
[*]Initial release.
[*]Carry weapons as sidearms, swap by gizmo.
[*]Auto-draw melee in melee, auto-restore the previous weapon afterwards.
[*]Optional auto-swap to a longer-ranged weapon.
[*]Raiders and other faction pawns use sidearms too, and gunners spawn with a melee weapon.
[/list]
